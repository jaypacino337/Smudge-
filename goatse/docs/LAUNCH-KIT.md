## Launch thread

**1/** 2,222 goats. Every one of the 134 layers is drawn in code — no image model, no anchor
sheet, no hand-nudging a fifth of the output. 🐐

**2/** Goats have horizontal rectangular pupils. That one detail is why these don't read as
a re-skinned dog collection, so every eye variant that isn't closed or transformed keeps the
bar.

**3/** Horns are the crown category. Fourteen silhouettes — nubs, scimitar, ibex, corkscrew,
markhor — all built from one taper primitive, so they stay in the same visual family while
being completely different shapes.

*[attach `output/sheets/07-horns.png`]*

**4/** Rarity lives in the filenames, so what you see is what the generator actually rolls.
Six headline traits are guaranteed exact: 8 laser eyes, 22 crystal horns, 22 crowns, 14
ghosts, 8 gilded, 22 pixel mode.

**5/** Fourteen exclusion rules stop the broken combinations from shipping — a cowboy hat
over ibex horns, eyewear over shut eyes, a black goat on a black background. Rejected and
rerolled, not published.

**6/** The whole thing is public: 134 layer definitions, the generator, the site, the prompt
library. Clone it and `npm run art` — twenty-five seconds and you have every layer.

## Things not to say

- No price talk, no "guaranteed", no floor predictions.
- No mint date until there is one.
- Don't claim the art is AI-generated — it isn't, and the prompt library existing makes that
  claim easy to misread. The Lab exists so the collection *could* be re-rendered in another
  medium; it is not how these were made.
- Don't call the supply 8,848. That number is the height of Everest and appears all over the
  site as the altitude motif. The supply is 2,222.
